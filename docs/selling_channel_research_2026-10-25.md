# Zero-cost selling channel research — 2026-10-25

Purpose: choose a selling route for ClubLedger Lite without paying setup or subscription fees. This is research only; no account was created and no listing published.

## Instamojo
- Official pricing page: https://www.instamojo.com/pricing
- Official help article for digital product links: https://support.instamojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208531995-How-to-create-a-digital-product-link
- Official help article for fees: https://support.instamojo.com/hc/en-us/articles/208482245-Instamojo-Transaction-Fees-No-Setup-or-Maintenance-Fees
- Their help page states digital products hosted by Instamojo cost 5% + ₹3 per transaction excluding 18% GST on the fee; another help article says hosted digital product links charge 5% + ₹3, while other product links with externally hosted files can use 2% + ₹3 excluding GST. The pricing page also displays a free Lite plan and lists 10% + ₹3 for digital products in one pricing table, so fee details are inconsistent across official pages/plans.
- Action: do not choose a plan or publish until the live account dashboard/support confirms the exact applicable fee and whether this standalone ZIP is accepted under the chosen product type.

## Gumroad
- Official pricing: https://gumroad.com/pricing
- Official payout guidance: https://gumroad.com/help/article/13-getting-paid
- Pricing page says no monthly charge, with 10% + $0.50 on direct/profile sales and 30% on Discover marketplace sales.
- Payout guidance says buyer payment methods (including UPI in India) are separate from creator payouts. Its current list of supported direct bank payout countries does not list India; do not assume buyer UPI support means the creator can receive payouts in India.
- Action: not the first choice until creator payout eligibility for an Indian account is verified.

## Recommendation
1. First validate demand manually with five relevant club/committee treasurers; no storefront or fees required.
2. If interest is real, confirm an India-eligible payout route and the exact transaction fees before choosing a platform.
3. Do not enter bank/UPI credentials into research notes, commit payment identifiers to Git, or claim revenue before a real payout is verified.
4. Current product status remains prototype; interactive browser QA pending; no public launch, orders, or verified revenue.

Research date: 2026-10-25. Fees and eligibility may change; confirm with the official provider before acting.
