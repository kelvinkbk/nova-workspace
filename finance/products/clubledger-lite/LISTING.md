# Product listing draft — ClubLedger Lite

**Working title:** ClubLedger Lite — Offline Student Club Finance Tracker

**Suggested launch price to test:** ₹49 (not yet published; price is an experiment, not a validated market rate).

**Short description:** Track club income, expenses, event spending and reserve funds in a simple offline app. No sign-up, subscription, or internet connection needed.

**What's included:**
- `index.html` — standalone browser app
- `README.md` — quick-start guide and limitations
- CSV export for spreadsheet use
- JSON backup and restore

**Who it's for:** Student clubs, campus committees, volunteer groups and small event teams that need a simple local ledger without setting up a spreadsheet from scratch.

**Honest limitations to disclose:** Local browser storage only; no multi-user sync; no bank or UPI integration; no independently verified accounting; prototype not yet broadly browser-tested.

**Zero-upfront launch checklist:**
1. Test the app in at least Chrome and Firefox with sample data; verify income/expense calculations, CSV export, JSON restore and delete confirmation.
2. Confirm a seller platform accepts the seller's country, payout method and this file type, and read its current fees before choosing it.
3. Publish only after the owner reviews the listing, price, payout details and platform terms. Do not pay for subscriptions or ads.
4. Track real orders and payouts separately in NOVA's evidence-based finance ledger. Never count clicks, pending orders, or demo entries as revenue.
5. If no one buys after a small, predefined test, gather feedback and improve the product rather than spending money to force sales.

**Launch status:** Draft only. No storefront has been created, and nothing has been published or sold.
